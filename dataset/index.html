CAPTure dataset

Single-host and multi-host corpus details

CAPTure contains 200 controlled CALDERA–Procmon scenarios: 100 single-host and 100 multi-host runs. Each setting contains 20 scenarios at Mix-2, Mix-3, Mix-4, Mix-5, and Mix-6.
